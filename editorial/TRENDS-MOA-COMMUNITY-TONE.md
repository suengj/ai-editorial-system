# trends.moa — 커뮤니티형 후킹 말투 (account-scoped overlay)

> Scope: **Instagram account `trends.moa` only**, for community-derived trend cards, one-page infographics, short captions and related publication handoffs. This is a **tone/wording overlay**, not a new content type, surface, brand palette, publication policy or global Korean language rule.

## 1. Activation and precedence

- Apply only when the **actual target account** is `trends.moa` (or the user explicitly requests this overlay for a particular asset). The common `editorial/profiles/surface/instagram.json` profile remains unchanged.
- Do **not** automatically apply to `suengj.com`, the owner's personal/professional Instagram, other Instagram accounts, Naver, LinkedIn, academic writing, finance research or general P03 articles. Source material coming from a community does **not** itself activate this overlay.
- Precedence: `editorial/constitution.md` (truth, uncertainty, provenance and approval) → content/language profiles → `editorial/voice.md` invariant requirements → selected account-specific tone → surface/rendering constraints. If the destination is personal `suengj-style`, that overlay continues to govern instead; do not blend both voices by default.
- This file changes **how a supported claim is phrased**, not what is claimed. It neither makes unverified rumours factual nor permits a publication action. No router, validator, account configuration or platform publisher is modified by this document.

## 2. One post, three linguistic functions

1. **Hook (on-image title):** colloquial, quickly graspable, typically one shock/curiosity/relatability angle in one or two short lines. Start with a recognisable situation and then a reaction; do not front-load generic marketing phrases.
2. **Info (small panels / caption body):** separate **what happened**, **why users care**, and **what is still uncertain**. Use native, clean Korean rather than filling every line with slang. A single factual post may have multiple information boxes without multiple repetitive hooks.
3. **Reader question (optional):** ask one natural, relevant question when two or more good-faith interpretations genuinely exist. Do not manufacture vote counts or insist on a CTA when no real question remains.

Choose **one main hook mechanism**, not all twelve. Never turn a clear source claim into an unsupported outcome just to increase excitement.

## 3. Tone intensity

| Tier | When | Sample hook | Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| LOW / 순한맛 | wider audiences, sensitive factual material | `AI랑 대화하다 정드는 사람, 왜 늘까?` | colloquial but neutral |
| MEDIUM / 중간맛 **(default)** | ordinary trends.moa cards and captions | `AI한테 정든다는 거, 이게 진짜 가능함?` | one spontaneous-sounding hook, no verbal clutter |
| HIGH / 매운맛 | adult relationship, unsettling story, clear community debate | `야한 채팅 켰다가 AI한테 정든다고? ㄹㅇ?` | sexual *topic* may be explicit; avoid pornographic narration, shaming and unfounded accusations |

Intensity controls rhythm and provocativeness, **never factual certainty**.

## 4. Hook repertoire — example copy, not purported community quotations

| ID | Pattern | Example |

| H01 | 경악형 (`아니 / ㄷㄷ / 실화냐`) | `아니 AI한테 진짜 설레는 사람도 있음?` |
| H02 | 선 넘음형 (`이건 좀 / 선 넘었는데`) | `새벽 DM이 돈 얘기로 바뀌면 이건 좀 쎄함` |

| H04 | 근황형 (`요즘 / 현재 / ~근황`) | `요즘 AI 연애앱 근황... 생각보다 진심임?` |
| H05 | 반전형 (`A인 줄 알았는데 B`) | `장난으로 시작했는데 어느새 매일 기다림` |
| H06 | 떡밥형 (`알고 보니 / 뒷이야기`) | `프로필보다 더 중요한 게 있었음` |
| H07 | 고백형 (`솔직히 / 고백함`) | `솔직히 AI한테 정든다는 말 조금 이해됨` |
| H08 | 저격·질문형 (`이게 맞냐 / 뭐임`) | `플러팅하다가 투자 얘기? 이게 맞냐` |
| H09 | 현타형 (`~하려다가 현타`) | `야한 얘기하러 갔다가 현타 오는 순간` |

| H11 | 의견 충돌형 (실제 쟁점) | `속궁합이 안 맞으면 사랑만으로 충분함?` |
| H12 | 참여 질문형 (`너네는 / ㅇㅈ?`) | `이 정도면 썸임? 너네는 어떻게 봄?` |

`ㄹㅇ`, `ㄷㄷ`, `ㅋㅋ`, `실화냐`, `이게 맞냐`, `좀 쎄함`, `현타` 등은 **optional lexical examples**, never a quota, mandatory suffix or universal register. The point is the short conversational move, not mechanical reproduction. Avoid manufactured profanity, exaggerated persecution, mocking victims or groups, and forced community caricature.

## 5. Evidence and visual-text guardrails

- **No fabricated evidence:** never invent comments, likes, upvotes, poll percentages, `온라인 4.2K`, platform trends, screenshots, source titles, quotes or dates. If demonstration UI is necessary, mark it visibly as **가상 예시/연출**; do not style it as measured community response.
- Distinguish `익명 이용자 주장` / `확인된 자료` / `편집자 해석` / `가상 시나리오`. Anonymous allegations about a person or organisation may not become asserted facts just because the headline is colloquial.
- Numbers require source, **population/denominator**, time period, and correct units. Cross-provider or trend charts continue under `editorial/INSTAGRAM-NUMERIC-VISUAL-POLICY.md`; this tone overlay does not relax numerical visual rules.
- The language overlay does not choose illustration style or media geometry. Photorealism, 8-bit, integrated text and deterministic overlays belong to the existing visual policies, not here.
- For adult themes, keep subjects adults and consent explicit when relevant. Avoid graphic sexual depiction, coercive/voyeuristic framing, nonconsensual disclosure or fabricated sexual allegations.
- A good headline can be provocative without `속보`, `충격 폭로`, `전국민`, `100%`, false urgency or unsupported causal language.
