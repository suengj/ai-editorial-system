# Rejected SUE-629 prototypes

Everything in this directory is owner-REJECTED output from the SUE-629
comparison gate. The owner rejection was recorded on 2026-09-07 in
[Linear SUE-629](https://linear.app/suengj/issue/SUE-629/aes-v216c-build-real-article-infographic-prototypes-and-run-owner).

It is retained ONLY as negative regression evidence for SUE-647. The SVG plates
demonstrate `dashboardization`, `ui_mimicry`, `flat_svg_aesthetic`, and
`box_overload`.

The `plans/*.json` files are instances of a composition-plan contract that was
deliberately NOT carried onto main. They must not be treated as a schema, a
contract, or a production default.

Nothing here may be used as a positive baseline.
