# 추론 가격 하락은 SaaS gross margin을 회복시키지 않는다

움직인 비용과 마진을 묶는 비용은 서로 다른 항목이다.

## 요점

- Prices fell and margins did not
- Why seat pricing amplifies rather than absorbs
- What would have to be true for the usual reading to hold

## 근거

- Published per-token inference prices for the mid-tier model fell over the period examined. — [Vendor pricing page](https://example.com/pricing)
- The filer's reported gross margin did not move materially over the same period. — [Quarterly filing](https://example.com/10q)
- Input tokens are billed on retrieval-augmented requests even when the generated output is short. — [Provider billing documentation](https://example.com/api-billing)

## 확인되지 않은 것

- Negotiated enterprise rates are not public; list prices overstate what large buyers pay.
- Serving-infrastructure cost is not separable from other COGS in most filings.
